Scope: the whole ColdLink web app (farmer search and booking, sign-in, provider onboarding and dashboard, provider action link). Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: Oyo State farmers on low-end Android phones, outdoors, booking cold storage after harvest; providers answering and managing bookings.

## Direction contract

THESIS: White enamel. The screen is a clean white basin; green fill bars measure what matters. Refuses the cream-and-serif, icon-bubble, rounded-card agri-app default.

OWN-WORLD: Pure white ground, green-black ink, ColdLink green (#17723f family) as the only brand colour: fill bars, selection, pill buttons. Amber and red only for state. Figtree throughout. Divided rows on white, hairline rules, no shadows, no icon circles, no tinted panels.

STORY: The farmer sees where they are, what they asked for and the exact naira price, and does one thing per screen.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Results on a phone: back arrow and title in a white bar; heading "6 cold rooms near Bodija" with the search summary; rows of cold rooms, each with name, distance and rating, the free space as a green number with a fill bar, the total in naira, and a green pill Book button.

FORM: Enamelware (impeccable's pick, recoloured green at the user's request), seed:a8997bbf7bfc3cf9. Signature move: green fill bars for space. The 3px green rule under headings was removed at the user's request (2026-11-26).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
